How to apply

  1. 1Login or register as a citizen.
  2. 2Choose a certificate and fill in the application form.
  3. 3Upload the required documents (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB each).
  4. 4Pay the application fee and submit.
  5. 5Track your application and download the certificate once approved.

Important information

  • Keep your application reference number safe. You need it to track your application.
  • Make sure uploaded documents are clear and readable.
  • Issued certificates can be downloaded any time from My Account.
